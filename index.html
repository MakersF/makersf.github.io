Francesco Zoffoli

Professional Software Engineer

Francesco Zoffoli is a professional Software Engineer, public speaker and book author, with experience in C++ and backend systems. He is passionate about programming languages, maintainable software and distributed systems.

Graduated in 2016 at Politecnico di Milano with a MSc in Computer Systems Engineering, he joined the industry working for Bloomberg LP building systems to enable collaboration of users. Since 2020 he’s been building software monitoring systems at Facebook, focussing on detection and alerting.
